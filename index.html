Applying Derivatives to Optimize Paddy Rice Development

Malaysia currently aims to achieve 100% Self-Sufficiency Level (SSL) for rice. Under the Muda Agricultural Development Authority (MADA) in Kedah, optimizing crop vegetative growth ensures high yields. By tracking plant height $f(x)$ over its 120-day cycle, the rate of change (first derivative) $f'(x)$ tells bioscience researchers exactly when the plant is accumulating biomass fastest. This crucial milestone marks the peak nutrient demand window, telling farmers precisely when to fertilize and flood fields.

Mathematical Modeling

1. Plant Growth Function, $f(x)$
f(x) = L / (1 + e-k(x - x₀))
Models standard sigmoidal growth. L is max height (cm), k is growth coefficient, and x₀ is growth inflection day.
2. Instantaneous Growth Rate, $f'(x)$
f'(x) = [L · k · e-k(x - x₀)] / (1 + e-k(x - x₀))2
Yields the daily rate of elongation (cm / day) at Day $x$ via the Chain Rule.
